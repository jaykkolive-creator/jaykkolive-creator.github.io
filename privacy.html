Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Onyx Analytics is a personal analytics tool built and operated by a single individual for his own use.

What we collect

When you authorize Onyx Analytics via a platform's official OAuth login (TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, or Instagram), we read only public profile statistics: display name, follower/subscriber count, and video/post count. We do not collect passwords, private messages, or any content beyond these public statistics.

How we use it

Retrieved statistics are displayed only in a private dashboard visible to the account owner and his moderators, so he can track his own channel growth over time. Data is never sold, shared, rented, or made accessible to any third party or advertiser.

Data retention and deletion

You may revoke Onyx Analytics' access at any time from the connected platform's own app/authorization settings, which immediately stops any further data collection. To request deletion of previously stored statistics, contact the account owner directly.
