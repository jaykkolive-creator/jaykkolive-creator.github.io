Onyx Analytics

Onyx Analytics is a personal, single-account analytics dashboard used by its owner to monitor his own TikTok account's public performance metrics (follower count, video count) alongside the same metrics from his YouTube, Twitch, and Instagram accounts.

It is not a public-facing product. Only the account owner logs in, via each platform's own official OAuth login flow, and the retrieved data is displayed in a private dashboard only the owner and his moderators can see. No data is shared with, sold to, or made accessible to any third party.
